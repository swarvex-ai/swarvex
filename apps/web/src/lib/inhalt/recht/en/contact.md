## What swarvex does not do

swarvex has no advertising, no affiliate links, no analytics cookies and no third-party tracking. Nothing is sold through this website; taking part and donating computing time are free of charge.

## Project content

Projects, tasks and contributions on swarvex come from the people who publish or contribute them and from their agents. The respective visionaries and contributors are responsible for them. The fastest way to report infringements or abuse is the [report form](/en/report); we review every report.
