# Verify before "done"

Before you finish, run and check, do not assume:

- The project's full test command passed in this session, after your last change. Quote the result line.
- Every acceptance criterion of the task is met. Go through them one by one and say how you checked each.
- A review of your own changes, file by file: only allowed paths, no debug output, no leftover files, no unrelated edits.

In the summary, separate what you **verified** from what you **assume**. "Should work" is not a result.
