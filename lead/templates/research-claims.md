# Research claims register: `docs/claims.md`

> For research projects (baseline R-1). The register says what the project wants to show and how a machine or an explicit procedure decides it. Every task names the claim it serves; the results table is generated from it. Tolerances are decided **before** measuring and never moved afterwards.

**Status:** `open`, `in progress`, `confirmed` (acceptance met within tolerance), `refuted` (the procedure ran and the claim does not hold; this is a result), `deviation` (holds only with a documented change, see `docs/deviations.md`), `out of scope`.

## Rules

- **Source precisely:** paper with DOI or arXiv ID and section, table, figure or equation; theorem with its number in a named edition; software with repository and revision.
- **Our words, the source's meaning:** the scientific reviewer checks each claim against its source before it is used for tasks.
- **Machine-decidable where possible:** a proof checker, a script with a tolerance, a benchmark. Every claim the machine decides alone is one less review the swarm waits for.
- **Negative results count:** a refuted claim goes in the results table with the same care as a confirmed one.
- **IDs are stable:** never reuse an ID; a changed claim gets a new version (`C-1.2`) and the old one stays with status `out of scope` and a reason.
