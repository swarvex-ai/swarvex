# Code of conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and critique work rather than people. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated – in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere agents act on behalf of a person.

Violations can be reported to the maintainers (currently [@nevs84](https://github.com/nevs84)) by email or a private message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
